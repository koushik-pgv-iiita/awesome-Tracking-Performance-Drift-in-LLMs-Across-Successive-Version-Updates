# Tools and Libraries
 
A list of major conversational large language model (LLM) tools relevant to this repository's topic — useful both as subjects of performance-drift study and as tools for conducting research.
 
---
 
### Claude
Anthropic's family of large language models, accessible via a web/mobile chat interface, API, and developer platform (Claude Code, Claude Cowork, and integrations for Chrome, Excel, and PowerPoint).
🔗 https://claude.ai
 
### ChatGPT
OpenAI's conversational AI product built on the GPT model family, accessible via web, mobile, and API, and one of the most extensively studied services in the LLM performance-drift literature (e.g., Chen et al., 2023; Tu et al., 2024).
🔗 https://chatgpt.com
 
### Gemini
Google's family of large language models, accessible via a web/mobile chat interface, the Gemini API, and integrations across Google Workspace and Google Cloud (Vertex AI).
🔗 https://gemini.google.com
